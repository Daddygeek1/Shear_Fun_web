Invasive Species

So there are these plants and animals that come from somewhere else and they just... take over everything. Like kudzu and Japanese knotweed. They're not from Missouri and they push out the stuff that IS from Missouri. It's actually pretty annoying. We made a whole page about it.

Endangered Species

This page is about the animals and plants in Missouri that are really close to disappearing. Like we're sorry. We didn't do a great job protecting them. This page tells you what they are and what we can do to help them stick around.

Bio-Glow

This is the fun one. Bioglow is about how some plants and animals actually light up! Like bioluminescence. It's basically nature's glow-in-the-dark and it's SO cool. We did a whole project on it.

Why We Made This

Our FLL challenge for this season (Bio Glow) is about the environment and taking care of nature. Bio Library is the 2026 team project, an online website created to provide the community with information about preserving Missouri’s biodiversity. The website helps people learn about Missouri’s plants, animals, ecosystems, and the importance of protecting the state’s natural environment.

Our mentor Steve helped us figure out the website stuff. We did the writing and the ideas. It took a while but we think it turned out pretty cool.
