Bio-Library

Bio Library is the 2026 team project, an online website created to provide the community with information about preserving Missouri's biodiversity. The website helps people learn about Missouri's plants, animals, ecosystems, and the importance of protecting the state's natural environment.

Invasive & Nuisance Species

Plants and animals that aren't from Missouri (or even ones that are, but cause problems) can really mess up the ecosystem. We broke this down into invasive plants, nuisance native plants, and problem animals.

Endangered Species

Some of Missouri's plants and animals are really close to disappearing. This page is about which ones, why, and what we can do to help.

Bio-Glow

One of our season tasks is "Flip the Rock" where the robot flips a LEGO rock to reveal an ecosystem underneath. Bio-Glow is all about the living stuff in that ecosystem.

About Us

We are a FIRST LEGO League team with seven members from across the St. Louis area. As a team, we compete in two main parts of FLL: the Robot Game and the Innovation Project.

The Robot Game

We build and program a LEGO robot to complete missions on a game board. We get 2 minutes and 30 seconds to earn as many points as possible. We use coding, teamwork, and problem-solving. Sometimes the robot does not work the way we want it to, so we have to test it, fix it, and try again. The Robot Game teaches us how to work together, be creative, and never give up.

The Innovation Project

We identify a problem related to the season's theme and develop a creative solution. We research the problem, come up with ideas, and work together to create a solution that could help make an impact. This year, our project is the Bio Library website.
